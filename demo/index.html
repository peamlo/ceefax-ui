A quick view of what the library delivers. The class markup you write is on the left; the real library stylesheet (../src/index.css) renders it on the right. One import, then plain class="…" — identical in Astro, Svelte, or hand-written HTML.

The whole config vocabulary

data-gap0 – 8spacing step (shared by every primitive)
data-cols2 · 3 · 4grid: up to N columns, then collapse
data-alignstart · center · end · baselinecluster cross-axis
data-siderightsidebar: rail on the trailing edge
data-maxcontent · layoutcenter: one page vs two
data-variantred · green · yellow · blue · …component colour (card, btn, key)
--cf-grid-mine.g. 16chescape hatch → intrinsic auto-fit grid
--cf-sidebar-sidee.g. 30chescape hatch → rail width
