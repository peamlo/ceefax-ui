Why the title's top row is all 🬭

The title is 9 pixel rows: 2 blank, 5 of letters, 2 blank. Split into cell rows of 3, the letters straddle them. Here is the W, with a line every 3 pixel rows: 

. . . . . . . . . .
. . . . . . . . . .
. . # . . . . # . .   ← letter row 1: the bottom third of cell row 1
───────────────────
. . # . . . . # . .
. . # . # # . # . .   ← letter rows 2–4: cell row 2
. . # # # # # # . .
───────────────────
. . # # . . # # . .   ← letter row 5: the top third of cell row 3
. . . . . . . . . .
. . . . . . . . . .

So cell row 1 only ever has its bottom blocks lit: 🬭 (16 + 32 = 48), 🬏 (16) or 🬞 (32). Cell row 3 only has its top blocks: 🬂 (1 + 2 = 3). All the letter detail is in row 2. The blank pixel rows are what give the letters room on the band.

stories/_mosaic.js builds these characters from pixel art (logo(), lettering(), rule()), with the same sum arithmetic you've been doing. So far every mosaic in the project has been generated like that, by code or by asking an agent for the characters. A live editor of the project's own is planned, with the zxnet teletext editor as its reference (ADR-0021).

Your turn: CF → CE

An E is an F with a bottom bar. Cut out of its block, that changes just the pixel row where the bar goes, so only the F's bottom cell row changes. Here are its pixels now and after: 

F (now)              E (after)
# . . # # # # #      # . . . . . . #
# # # # # # # #      # # # # # # # #
# # # # # # # #      # # # # # # # #

These are the last four characters of the logo's bottom row, the ones under the F (🬺 🬻 █ █). The logo is white blocks with the letters carved out, so # is a lit block and . a dark one. Cut the E's rows into 4 cells, 2 pixels wide each: 

cell 1    cell 2    cell 3    cell 4
# .       . .       . .       . #
# #       # #       # #       # #
# #       # #       # #       # #

Cell 1, worked through:

  1. lit: top-left 1, middle 4 + 8, bottom 16 + 32
  2. sum 1 + 4 + 8 + 16 + 32 = 61
  3. range 43–62: 61 − 3 = 58
  4. 58 = 3 × 16 + 10 = 0x3A → U+1FB3A

That's already the first code point in the box: cell 1 is the same in the F and the E. Do cells 2, 3 and 4 the same way. Hint: cell 2 is cell 1 without its top-left block.

The box starts with the F's current code points. Write U+1FB39, 1FB39 or &#x1FB39;, whichever you like:

🬝🬎🬎🬬 🬝🬎🬎🬬 ▌▐██ ▌🬠🬰█ 🬺🬹🬹🬻 🬺🬻██

Typing a code point into source

Pasting the characters is what the repo does, but you can also write the number itself. Both of these give 🬹:

inwrite
HTML&#x1FB39; (&#x, the hex, ;)
JavaScript'\u{1FB39}' or String.fromCodePoint(0x1fb39)

For a one-cell fix that's quicker than redrawing. New art is hundreds of cells: generate it from pixel art, as stories/_mosaic.js does, or, once it exists, draw it in the project's live editor.
